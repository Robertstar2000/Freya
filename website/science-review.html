Evidence and calculation findings

BIO-01 · Critical · Interpretation

Retained radiolabel is not a life verdict. The eight C/H channels are schematic tracer experiments, not balanced reactions; abiotic fixation, sorption, isotope exchange and contamination can also retain label.

Required disposition: Require time-zero, reagent/no-soil, matrix, killed and recovery controls plus an orthogonal molecular/product measurement before a biological interpretation.

BIO-02 · High · Energy source wording

Outline A(e) says rare gases. Antarctic evidence concerns trace H2/CO, not noble gases. It does not establish a preferred Martian metabolism or rule out phototrophy.

Required disposition: Keep the original wording in the source record; use trace-gas hypothesis in the design and obtain author clarification.

BIO-03 · High · Water and incubation

Liquid water, water activity, salinity, temperature and pressure are separate constraints. A universal Martian threshold is unknown; water activity cannot simply be omitted. Incubation can alter the native state.

Required disposition: Log chamber/sample temperature, pressure, hydration and gas history. Validate recovery under each chosen condition and qualify negative results accordingly.

BIO-04 · Critical · Duration and power

The gas/trap source proposes 1–5 sols. A 24-hour goal is shorter than one Mars sol; B 72 hours is shorter than five. The 20 Wh / 0.2 W screen does not establish heater, pumping, counting or reagent-actuation performance.

Required disposition: Resolve assay duration versus mission lifetime; build a time-resolved, thermally derated power and energy budget. Live arithmetic below flags the mismatch.

BIO-05 · High · Dual-label inference

Raw pulse-height or photon-burst ratios do not directly equal the 3H/14C activity ratio. Beta spectra overlap; quenching, exchangeable tritium, recovery and spectral spillover can change the apparent ratio.

Required disposition: Calibrate single-isotope standards, mixtures, blanks and matrix effects; propagate the response-matrix uncertainty. A fraction-dependent ratio is a candidate signature, not unique proof of reductive anabolism.

BIO-06 · High · Nitrogen and sulfur interpretation

C/H labels in an amine- or thiol-reactive fraction do not by themselves prove N2 or SO2 supplied its nitrogen or sulfur. -3/-2 are relevant reduced groups, not universal oxidation states for all biomass atoms.

Required disposition: Resolve the identity of phenylcyanate versus possible isocyanate wording; validate capture specificity and reagent compatibility. Use orthogonal elemental/isotopic attribution in ground validation.

BIO-07 · High · SiPM operating principle

The outline says below breakdown. Geiger-mode microcells operate above breakdown and are quenched after avalanches. The cited Failla paper addresses linearity, not an assay-specific tritium detection limit.

Required disposition: Specify bias/temperature compensation, photon coupling, dark noise, cross-talk, dead time, saturation, low-energy beta geometry and in-matrix efficiency.

BIO-08 · High · Extraction and sampling

Water and toluene are operational fractions, not perfectly exclusive chemical classes. A 100 nm filter does not prove sterile separation or analyte recovery. One gram and 0.25 cm3 are not equivalent without density. Nose penetration does not prove sampled source depth.

Required disposition: Measure sample mass and provenance, carryover, filter retention, extraction recovery and gas/solid/liquid label balance. Demonstrate access to the stated 2–5 cm zone independently of the penetration screen.

BIO-09 · High · Oxidant discrimination

Parent perchlorate can be kinetically slow while irradiation products remain reactive. Neither universally reactive nor universally self-sterilizing soil follows. GEx O2 provenance remains an interpretation question.

Required disposition: Treat peroxide, superoxide, oxychlorine and molecular O2 as competing/overlapping targets. Validate selectivity in minerals and salts; distinguish adsorbed, trapped and newly generated O2.

BIO-10 · High · Optical assay specificity

410/560 nm and proposed NBT/hydroethidine/O2 indicators are candidate readouts, not unique species identifications. Matrix color, pH, metals, turbidity and cross-reactions can interfere.

Required disposition: Require matrix-matched blanks, recovery standards, kinetic/spectral controls and validated identification of the specific product, particularly 2-hydroxyethidium.

BIO-11 · High · Statistics and detection limits

No measured count histories, background distribution, calibration, extraction yield, LOD/LOQ, biological replication or false-positive targets are supplied. More probes alone do not determine life-detection probability.

Required disposition: Predeclare endpoints and error rates; separate independent samples from repeated counts; establish detection capability using blank and low-level validation data. No assay sensitivity or life probability is computed here.

BIO-13 · High · Detector thermal limits

The consulted C-Series datasheet recommends operation from −40 to +85 °C. Ambient Mars or heat-shield model temperatures do not establish detector temperature or gain stability.

Required disposition: Provide an electronics enclosure thermal model and measured detector temperature/bias calibration; do not assume a room-temperature sensitivity applies in flight.

BIO-12 · Medium · Flight readiness

A reagent/device trade and laboratory outline are not a packaged, impact-qualified payload. Planetary protection, contamination controls, sealing, storage stability and post-impact calibration remain open.

Required disposition: Complete component mass/volume boundaries and integrated environmental, shock, contamination and sample-transfer verification before flight selection.

Experiment and instrument trades

Eight C/H tracer alternatives

  1. ¹⁴CO₂ + H₂: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  2. CO₂ + ³H₂: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  3. ¹⁴CO + H₂: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  4. CO + ³H₂: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  5. ¹⁴CO₂ + H₂O: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  6. CO₂ + ³H₂O: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  7. ¹⁴CO + H₂O: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.
  8. CO + ³H₂O: compare retained activity in independently recovered fractions with matched blanks and recovery controls.

Superscripts denote isotopes (³H is tritium, not a stoichiometric coefficient). These expressions label candidate input channels; they do not specify balanced net reactions, metabolic yields or universal energy sources. Water is an assay condition and isotope-exchange partner, not by itself an identified reducing-energy source.

Indirect N/S and dual-label extensions

Amine/thiol fraction capture and hydrophobic/hydrophilic partitioning can support a multi-measurement argument after specificity validation. They cannot alone establish the nitrogen/sulfur source, biosynthetic identity or biology. Unlabeled H₂/CO response comparisons need substrate and sorption controls; an increased yield does not by itself identify a metabolic lifestyle.

Measurement equations and missing inputs

Count correction: net rate=N_sample/t_sample−N_blank/t_blank. For independent Poisson counts only, Var(net rate)=N_sample/t_sample²+N_blank/t_blank². Correlated drift, quenching and blank variability require more than this counting-noise model.

Activity and mass normalization: recovered activity=net rate/efficiency; input-normalized activity additionally needs independently measured extraction recovery and initial activity. Report per measured soil mass, not a volume-to-mass guess. Neither detector efficiency nor recovery is supplied, so the site does not compute a biological yield.

Two-isotope response: measured channel-rate vector r=M A+b, with isotope activities A, blank b and calibrated efficiency/spillover matrix M. Inferring A requires an identifiable, adequately conditioned response matrix and propagated calibration uncertainty. Negative noisy estimates are censored/qualified under a predeclared rule, not silently called zero activity.

Label accounting: compare activity in gas, solid, liquid fractions, surfaces and losses after decay and calibration corrections. Missing activity is not evidence of biomass. Exchangeable tritium and residual inorganic carbon require specific controls.

Detection claims: LOD/LOQ, blank limits, false-positive/false-negative targets, replicate counts, incubation endpoint and decision rules remain unspecified. No measured assay result, yield, detector sensitivity or life-detection probability is produced by this website.

Engineering and uncertainty review limits

Current automated checks cover orbital and rocket identities, all three cold-gas withdrawals, staged mass conservation, entry/descent refinement, RF and energy arithmetic, impact work integration and log-depth uncertainty sensitivities. Algebraic closure does not validate hardware. In particular, the penetration screen currently uses complete probe mass as the effective moving mass; a separated surface aftbody would require a different coupled impact model. Sample acquisition, specimen temperature, assay chemistry and post-impact function are not solved.

Depth shading is conditional first-order lognormal propagation with analyst-selected input factors, not empirical scatter or a bioscience detection probability. Targeting and heating remain reduced engineering models. Historical PDFs and studies retain their earlier inputs; not every archived calculation was independently rederived.
