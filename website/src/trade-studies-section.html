SCIENCE / NOVEMBER 12 BENNER OUTLINE

Science package trades

The outline proposes preflight evaluation of isotope-tracer extraction and an alternative oxidant investigation for the 2028 precursor. Candidate studies carry the Rev 10 contamination, control, telemetry and power requirements through to acceptance evidence.

Reductive anabolism and extraction

Compare the gas-trap and solvent-extraction concepts, direct carbon fixation and proposed nitrogen or sulfur incorporation readouts. Trade aqueous, hydrophobic, surfactant-assisted and scintillation-compatible fractions on recovery, carryover, containment and separation of abiotic backgrounds.

Compare matrix blanks, inactivated controls, reference spikes and representative simulants. Use measured sample recovery and tracer accounting, including isotope exchange, before assigning a cause to excess counts.

Detector and isotope separation

Candidate SiPMs include onsemi MICROFC-60035-SMT and MICROFC-30035, Broadcom AFBR-S4N44C013 and the Eikonal Optics SIPM-01 module. Compare response matrix, background, cross-talk, recovery, live time and readout energy. Evaluate mixed-label discrimination with calibrated references.

2028 alternate oxidant screen

Compare peroxide, superoxide and molecular-oxygen indicator channels on selectivity, instrument burden and false positives in representative matrices. Require controls for overlapping optical responses and soil-mineral interference. Report chemical evidence with its uncertainties.
